Calculation model: Daily Rate is read-only from the Employee record's Daily Rate custom field. Hourly Rate = Daily Rate ÷ Hours/Day. Basic Pay = Daily Rate × Actual Days Duty. OT Pay = Hourly Rate × OT Hours. Holiday Pay = Daily Rate × Holiday Factor. Allowances and Commissions are manual earnings. Late Deduction = Hourly Rate × Late Hours. Tardiness is calculated and posted as a deduction using the dedicated Tardiness Payslip Item. SSS, PHIC, Pag-IBIG, withholding tax, and loans/advances remain manual deductions. Employer contributions remain manual.

Assumptions for this prototype: Daily Rate is read from the Employee custom field named "Daily Rate"; 8 hours/day; overtime uses the regular hourly rate. Holiday Factor is entered per employee (for example 1.00 for one additional day or 0.30 for a 30% premium). The Daily Rate is read-only here for verification. These are calculation settings, not a determination of Philippine statutory compliance. Payslip writing uses the Payslip Item mappings confirmed in this Manager business.
